The lead of a `Section` is set in the prose family and its elegant weight, prose/xl from `md` and prose/large on phones. The lead of the `Hero` takes the colour of the band at 80% opacity in place of `fg-subtle`. A card of `Cards` has a border, transparent until hover, when the card takes the primary background and border; both fade in. The body of a card fills its height, so the action lines of a row align.
